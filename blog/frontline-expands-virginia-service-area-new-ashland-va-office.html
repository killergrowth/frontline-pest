Frontline Expands Virginia Service Area with New Ashland, VA Office

Frontline Pest Control is opening a new location in Ashland, VA, growing its presence throughout the greater Richmond area. The new office will allow Frontline's licensed, local technicians to provide faster, more responsive general pest, termite, rodent, and mosquito control service to homeowners in and around Ashland and Hanover County.

As part of Frontline's continued expansion across the Mid and South Atlantic, the Ashland office reflects the company's commitment to bringing hyper-local pest expertise directly to the neighborhoods it serves. Residents in the area can now schedule same-day service, take advantage of current special offers, and work with a dedicated local team backed by Frontline's 100% satisfaction guarantee.
