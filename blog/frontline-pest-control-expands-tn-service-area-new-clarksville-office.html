Frontline Pest Control Expands TN Service Area With New Clarksville Office

Frontline Pest Control is opening a new location in Clarksville, TN, expanding pest, termite, rodent, and mosquito control to more homeowners across Middle Tennessee. This marks continued growth for Frontline's Tennessee footprint, which already includes offices serving Nashville, Brentwood, Madison, and Murfreesboro.

The Clarksville office will be staffed by local, licensed technicians trained on Frontline's full 18 aspects of service, bringing same-day scheduling and family-friendly treatments to the region.
