Frequently Asked Questions

What methods do you use for rodent control, and how effective are they?

We use a combination of methods tailored to the specific rodent species and the severity of the infestation, including bait stations, traps, exclusion techniques, and habitat modification recommendations. Our integrated approach focuses on long-term control, not just a quick fix.

Is the treatment safe for my family, pets, and the environment?

The safety of your family, pets, and the environment is our top priority. We use EPA-approved products and follow strict safety protocols, and we'll inform you of any necessary precautions.

How can I prepare my home for a rodent control treatment?

It's helpful to remove clutter, especially in areas where rodents have been sighted, store food in airtight containers, and keep pet food off the ground.

How long does the treatment typically last?

Duration depends on the severity of the initial infestation, treatment type, and property conditions. We typically recommend ongoing preventative measures and offer various maintenance plans for long-term control.
