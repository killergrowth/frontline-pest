How Our Mosquito Control Service Works

Mosquitoes and ticks can transform your outdoor spaces from family gathering areas into health hazards, carrying diseases like West Nile virus and Lyme disease. Our comprehensive approach addresses both adult populations and breeding sites through expert assessment and targeted treatment.

Our Certified Technicians' Proven Mosquito Process

  1. Professional Property Assessment by certified technicians who identify breeding sites and activity patterns
  2. Targeted Foliage Treatment of non-flowering vegetation up to 15 feet
  3. Residual Barrier Application creating long-lasting protection zones around your property
  4. Larvicide Treatment of standing water sources to break breeding cycles
  5. Bi-monthly Professional Service during peak season (March-October)

Tick Control: An Added Layer of Protection

Our specialists also target disease-carrying ticks that transmit Lyme disease and Rocky Mountain spotted fever, providing comprehensive protection in a single treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I prepare my yard for a mosquito control treatment?

Eliminate standing water sources around your property, such as in flower pots, bird baths, and clogged gutters, and trim overgrown vegetation before our technicians arrive.

Where do mosquito technicians spray?

We treat all non-flowering foliage around your home, including areas where these bugs flock together like patios, fire pits, or around water features.

Are your mosquito treatments safe for my pets?

Yes. Frontline uses products that are organic and safe for animals, plants, and children, targeting mosquitoes specifically.
