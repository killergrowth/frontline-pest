Frequently Asked Questions

Are your termite treatments safe for my family?

Yes! The solution we use to treat termites is safe for pets, kids, and plants. The treatment stations are in-ground and enclosed.

What does a termite inspection include?

We offer a free termite inspection. One of our technicians will visually inspect for any evidence of termites, assess whether current conditions are conducive to termite activity, and provide recommendations on treatments or preventive measures.

How do your termite treatments work?

We install pre-baited in-ground stations roughly every 10 feet apart around the exterior of a property, for active termite cases or as a preventative measure. During your annual renewal inspection, all stations are checked for activity and replaced if needed.

When should I call a termite exterminator?

You should call Frontline at the first sign of termite activity. It is best to treat immediately to prevent further damage to your home.
